About me

I am currently a Ph.D. student in The Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Florida, advised by Dr. Ivan Ruchkin, who leads the Trustworthy Engineered Autonomy (TEA) Lab. I earned my master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Florida in May 2025. Previously, I pursued graduate coursework in Electrical and Systems Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania. I received my bachelor's degree in Microelectronics Science and Engineering from the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), China, in 2022, where I was also a member of Pan Group@SUSTech and studied high-speed communication interfaces with Dr. Quan Pan. I also interned at the Brain-inspired Computing Lab at the Guangzhou campus of the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST-GZ), where I worked on spiking neural networks (SNNs) and event cameras under the supervision of Dr. Renjing Xu.

Research Interests:
  • Safe Autonomous Systems and LLM Agents: Modeling, analysis, monitoring, and prediction; confidence measurement for reliable autonomous systems and LLM agents.
  • Uncertainty Quantification and Temporal Confidence Calibration: in large language models (LLMs), vision-language models (VLMs), and vision-language-action models (VLAs).
  • Physically Interpretable World Models: representation learning and world models that support physically meaningful prediction.

Selected Builds
  • SUSTech Tutoring Booking System: Built and operated the full-stack Java/Spring Boot backend and the WeChat Mini Program frontend. The system was used by approximately one-third of undergraduates, with hundreds of reservations.
  • AutoGators: Built autonomous convoy routing that combines drone imagery, threat detection, and graph-based planning with an AWS-backed Streamlit/Folium operator interface. The project received the Most Innovative Solution Award (1st Place) at the 2025 AWS & Vanderbilt Mission Autonomy Hackathon.
  • LoreJump: Co-founded and built the backend of a university knowledge-sharing platform with REST APIs and a recommendation engine over user-generated knowledge graphs, 2020–2021.

Ongoing Projects
  • Hallucination Detection in Video Foundation Models: Developing a unified hallucination detection framework for state-of-the-art text-to-video generative models (e.g., Wan, Hunyuan Video). The project focuses on multimodal consistency checking, motion/physics violation detection, and reference-free evaluation metrics for identifying temporal and spatial hallucinations in generated videos.
  • Uncertainty Quantification & Calibration in Large Foundation Models: Investigating confidence estimation, temporal uncertainty modeling, and calibration techniques for LLMs and VLMs. The project aims to build reliable uncertainty-aware reasoning pipelines for different tasks and multimodal understanding.
  • Reliable World Models for Autonomous Systems: Building reliable world models by integrating formal verification and calibration methods to ensure consistent dynamics prediction, robustness under distribution shifts, and trustworthy deployment.
